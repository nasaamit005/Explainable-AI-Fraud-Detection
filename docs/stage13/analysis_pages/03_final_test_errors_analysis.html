Final Test — False Positives and False Negatives

← Back to Stage 13 dashboard

Objective

Compare missed fraud cases with legitimate transactions incorrectly flagged.

Methodology

Count confusion-matrix errors at each model's validation-selected threshold.

Interpretation

The relative importance of false negatives and false positives depends on real operational costs and review capacity.

Limitations

Counts alone do not determine business value. Monetary loss, investigation costs, and review capacity were not provided.
